# SEO + GEO Work — Client-Friendly Summary

**For:** The Himalayan Shire · **Date:** 2026-09-11

## What was improved?

Your website now explains itself far more clearly — to Google and to AI assistants. Every page has its own proper title and description (previously five pages all showed the homepage title). Your property details — name, Fagu address, phones, the seven rooms, pet rules, distances to Shimla and Kufri — are now stated identically everywhere, including in the hidden machine-readable labels Google and AI systems read. We added a snowfall/winter guide, a private-villa page for group bookings, and a simple text file (`llm.txt`) that gives AI systems verified facts and links instead of guesses.

## Why does it matter?

When titles are duplicated or facts disagree across pages, Google struggles to understand and trust the site. Consistent facts plus structured labels make it easy for search engines to match your pages to what travellers actually type — "homestay in Fagu", "rooms near Shimla", "snow stay", "pet-friendly stay".

## What can search engines understand better now?

Which page is about what (rooms vs villa vs pets vs activities), where exactly you are (Fagu, 19 km from Shimla, 5 km from Kufri), what each room offers, and which questions each page answers.

## What can AI systems understand better now?

The same, plus a verified fact sheet they can quote: your rooms with links, your real policies, your real guides, and your official contact paths — reducing the chance of AI inventing details about you.

## What still needs monitoring?

Google Search Console (indexing, queries, page experience) and your Google Business Profile (details, photos, reviews). These live outside the website and only you can own them.

## What do you need to do?

1. In Search Console: verify the site, submit the sitemap, ask Google to index the new villa and snowfall pages.
2. On your Business Profile: confirm name, address, phone, categories, pet-friendly attribute, photos, and booking link.
3. Collect genuine guest reviews and reply to them.
4. When rooms, prices, policies, or contact details change, tell us so the fact sheet (`llm.txt`) stays accurate.
5. Confirm the blog author name shown on posts belongs to the real writer.

## One honest note

No one — us included — can promise Google rankings or that ChatGPT or Gemini will mention you. What we built is the foundation that makes you *eligible* to be found and correctly understood. Visibility itself builds over time through indexing, reviews, and genuine mentions elsewhere.
