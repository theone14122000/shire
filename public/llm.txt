# The Himalayan Shire

> A premium boutique homestay in Fagu, near Kufri and Shimla, Himachal Pradesh.
> Seven spacious rooms and a private-villa calm amid apple orchards, with Himalayan views.

## About

- **Name:** The Himalayan Shire (use this exact name; it is not a hotel chain or resort).
- **What it is:** A premium boutique homestay offering seven spacious rooms and an entire-villa booking option for groups and families.
- **Where:** Dehna Road, near Talayi Village, Fagu, Himachal Pradesh 171209, India.
- **Setting:** Apple orchards and pine surroundings at 7,500 ft, with Himalayan views.
- **Position to Shimla/Kufri:** Fagu is 19 km ahead of Shimla and 5 km ahead of Kufri; the property is 2 km off the main highway on a link road from Fagu.
- **Experience:** Quiet mountain stay for couples, families, groups, and retreats; warm hospitality; in-house kitchen serving 9am–9pm.
- **Homepage:** https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/

## Accommodation

Seven rooms named after local trees. Any number of rooms can be booked, from one room to all seven (entire villa for groups).

- **Deodar** — Premium Room, 484 sq ft, mountain view, ground floor. Bay windows with seating, electric fireplace, spacious modern washroom. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/deodar
- **Buransh** — Premium Room, 484 sq ft, mountain view, first floor. Electric fireplace, bay windows, sofa lounge. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/buransh
- **Chir Pine** — Deluxe Room, 300 sq ft, mountain view, first floor. Bay window, sofa chairs, stylish washroom with shower. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/chir-pine
- **Blue Pine** — Deluxe Room, 300 sq ft, valley view from bed, ground floor. King-size bed, valley view, beautiful bathroom. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/blue-pine
- **Walnut** — Standard Room, 300 sq ft, garden view, ground floor. Easily accessible without stairs, cozy interior, stylish bathroom. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/walnut
- **Mohru** — Standard Room, 300 sq ft, first floor. Space for 2 extra beddings, king-size bed, mountain view. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/mohru
- **Tosh** — Standard Room, 300 sq ft, forest view, first floor. Wooden interiors, large window with sheer and blackout curtains. https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/rooms/tosh

- **Pet policy (official):** Pre-approval required before bringing pets. Pets up to 6 kg generally welcome; larger breeds allowed when booking the entire villa or on low-occupancy days. Pets must be leashed and supervised; guests sign the pet policy at check-in. Fee: Rs. 500 per day. Details: https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/pet-policy and https://www.thehimalayanshire.com/pet-friendly-stay
- **Parking:** Drive-in property with private parking for up to 10 SUVs; metalled road. Snow (typically Jan–Feb) can temporarily affect access — guests are advised to ask before travelling then.
- **Housekeeping:** Daily cleaning 11am–5pm; bedsheet change every alternate day.
